## Topics covered

JOINS were my main focus, with extra practice completed at [Data Lemur](https://datalemur.com/).

## New Skills

Thinking about how Left, Right, Inner and Outer Joins are structured, what exactly they do and how they can be used has been really beneficial.
This plus the challenges I completed at Data Lemur have really helped me strengthen my basic query skills.

Adding MySQL to my device will also help me going forward, and taught me a lot about how the system itself works.

## Challenges

This week I decided to move away from BigQuery, Google's native database management system recommended by their data analysis course, and towards MySQL. I decided to do this as MySQL is open source,
and I am not constrained by the limits oof the free version of BigQuery.

However, actually installing the software onto my device and uploading my practice dataset was my biggest challenge this week. BigQuery streamlines the upload process a lot more, so I really had to
gain a stronger understanding of what it is that is actually happening when a dataset is uploaded to MySQL from a csv file, knowledge that will be useful in the future. I'm a big football fan, so I
decided to upload [this dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rezagooner/comprehensive-english-premier-league-match-dataset) from Kaggle, containing English Premier League data from 2000 to 2026.
However, the sheer amount of columns I had to set up in order to upload the data was a challenge. I'm trying to avoid the use of AI software as a crutch during my learning process, but asking ChatGPT 
to format all 65 columns and then just checking its work really streamlined this process.

I also really enjoyed the Data Lemur challenges I completed for further JOIN practice. I had to think outside the box to complete them, which gave me a taste of what my future career will hopefully entail.
